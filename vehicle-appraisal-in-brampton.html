FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

When does Service Ontario require an appraisal?

An appraisal is required when a vehicle's wholesale book value cannot be determined, when the vehicle is 20 years old or older, and in gift or family-transfer situations. Retail sales tax is then calculated on the appraised value instead of the purchase price or book value.

How long does an appraisal take?

Most are completed in a single visit of about 15 to 30 minutes, and you leave with the signed document the same day.

What does an appraisal cost?

It depends on the vehicle and the purpose. Call (437) 291-1665 for a quick quote — no obligation.

What should I bring?

The vehicle, its ownership (the green permit), your driver's licence, and any service records you have. If an office or insurer requested the appraisal, bring their paperwork too.

Do you appraise classic cars and motorcycles?

Yes. Classic, antique and collector vehicles are a large part of appraisal work, because standard book values rarely reflect their real market value. Motorcycles are appraised as well.
